Using KIM6

Install KIM6, restart Dolphin, then select one or more local images or videos. Right-click and choose an action from a KIM6 menu. The five menus group photo compression, resizing and sharing, conversion and rotation, video tools, and publishing tasks.

Many actions let you create a new file or replace the selected one. Choose Create new to keep the original. If your saved preference selects overwrite, use a disposable copy when trying an action. Web export, email copies, video frames and audio, GIF exports, and slideshows always create separate results.

See Install KIM6 or try the everyday examples.

Photos

Resize or compress

Choose a maximum size or resize by percentage. A 600 × 600 size keeps the photo's proportions and fits it inside that box; it does not crop the image into a square. JPEG compression changes encoding quality, not pixel dimensions. Lower quality can lose detail.

Convert, rotate, or flip

Convert images to formats such as JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, AVIF, HEIC/HEIF, JPEG XL, or JPEG 2000 when your installed tools support them. Some formats lose transparency, layers, animation, or image detail. Rotation and flipping change the selected image only when you choose an authorized replacement.

Sharing photos

Web export makes a resized JPEG, WebP, or AVIF copy in a folder you select. It never replaces the source photo. Its default color option converts a usable embedded profile to sRGB; you can choose to keep the source profile instead.

Email actions can prepare smaller copies or attach selected originals at full size. They do not change the selected files. Do not send a test draft unless you intend to send it.

Metadata and photo dates

You can remove all metadata or remove recognized GPS coordinates while leaving other information in place. GPS-only removal does not guarantee that every possible location clue is gone. You can also add creator and copyright details or adjust camera capture times.

Date tools distinguish the time written inside a photo from the file's modification time. A time shift changes the recorded capture time by a fixed amount; it is not a time-zone conversion.

Albums and collections

Create an HTML gallery, a photo collage, a PDF album, a print-layout PDF, a multi-page TIFF, or an animated GIF from selected images. These actions create new results; choose the destination or filename shown by the dialog.

An MP4 slideshow is a simple video of selected photographs in natural filename order. It has a fixed display time for each photo and no music or transitions.

Video

Video tools can resize and encode a copy using H.264, H.265, or an available AV1 encoder. Other actions extract a frame, make a contact sheet, save an audio track, remove audio without re-encoding the video stream, or make an animated GIF from a short segment.

Video format and encoder availability depend on your system. An action reports when a requested codec or container is unavailable rather than silently choosing a different one.

Troubleshooting

The menus do not appear

Restart Dolphin and check that the five service-menu files were installed in the expected folder. KDE Frameworks/KIO 6.29 has a known upstream issue affecting submenu display. KIM6 keeps its submenus; do not remove them as a workaround. See installation help.

A format or codec is unavailable

KIM6 uses the image and video tools installed on your system. Optional codecs and format support vary. See format and codec notes.

Where can I find more help?

Read the Getting Started guide, the safe handling guide, or the installation instructions. Include the menu action and a short description of the problem when reporting an issue. Do not send private photographs unless you have permission.
